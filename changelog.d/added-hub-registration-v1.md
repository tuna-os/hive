- Added the hub registration protocol v1 (`/api/hub/v1`): a discovery document with limits and proof methods, self-service registrations as resources with ETag and `If-Match`, and `PATCH /api/hub/v1/registrations/{id}` to change repos and `dashboard_url` without re-keying the spoke. The per-registration repo cap is now configurable with `HIVE_HUB_SELFSERVICE_MAX_REPOS` (default 20).
