- The tuna fork's revert sheriff no longer leaves a held revert open without `hold`. The label can only be added after the pull request exists, and a failed label write was only logged, so the revert stayed open and mergeable while its text and the audit log said it was held. A held revert is now opened as a draft and marked ready only once its labels are on it; if they cannot be added it stays a draft and the audit log records `failed` with its number.
